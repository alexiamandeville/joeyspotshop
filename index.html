About the Artist

Joey Deien is a ceramic artist and cactus collector originally from San Antonio, Texas, now living and working in Los Angeles. Joey first found clay through local classes back in San Antonio, where the love for wheel throwing and handmade pottery began. These days, Joey creates work in Culver City, shaping each piece by hand and focusing on simple forms, thoughtful details, and glazes that bring personality to everyday objects.

Each piece of pottery is crafted with care, perfect for plant lovers and cactus collectors alike.
